What this assumes. That the share keeps wobbling at a steady rate and never gaps overnight on news. Real shares gap constantly, so far-away targets are slightly understated and near ones slightly overstated. Notice that changing volatility moves the answer far more than changing your directional view — if you express this with options, you are mostly trading volatility, not direction.

Connecting the live feeds

Every panel calls one of your own /api/ routes, so keys stay server-side and nothing hits a third party from the browser. Until the routes are deployed, panels show "not connected" rather than placeholder data.

Cost. Finnhub's free tier covers quotes and company news. SEC EDGAR needs no key at all — just a User-Agent header containing your email, which is their access requirement. StockTwits is free but rate-limited, so cache it. X/Twitter is deliberately absent: it removed its free tier in February 2026 and now bills per post read.
