# Image Generation Prompts

The raster assets were generated with the built-in image-generation workflow. These prompts can be reused to create later variants while preserving the pack's visual language.

## Hero — AI campus at dusk

```text
Use case: photorealistic-natural
Asset type: TimeToCompute homepage cinematic hero background
Primary request: a spectacular, believable aerial view of a vast modern AI data-centre campus at dusk, designed as the visual foundation for an editorial technology homepage about the physical infrastructure behind AI
Scene/backdrop: wide hyperscale campus stretching toward the horizon, multiple low rectangular data halls, electrical substations, transformers, cooling equipment, service roads, distant power pylons, a warm sunset fading into a deep charcoal sky
Subject: the real physical scale of an AI data-centre campus; operational buildings glowing subtly while one distant phase remains under construction
Style/medium: photorealistic premium editorial photography, credible industrial architecture, natural aerial-drone perspective, not science fiction
Composition/framing: very wide 16:9 landscape; strongest buildings and light concentration in the right and central areas; clean dark negative space across the left third for overlaid headline and CTA
Lighting/mood: dramatic blue hour, warm practical lights, restrained electric-lime reflections only in a few power conduits
Constraints: no text, logos, corporate branding, people or watermark; no fantasy skyline or excessive neon
```
