## Lead-story frame

**Eyebrow**

> THE PHYSICAL RACE BEHIND AI

**Dynamic headline pattern**

> [RECOGNISABLE CUSTOMER OR OPERATOR] [PLAIN-ENGLISH VERIFIED ACTION]

Current suitable example:

> MICROSOFT ACCEPTS IREN'S FIRST 50 MW AI DEPLOYMENT

**Plain-English consequence**

> One major delivery risk has been removed. Billing has not yet been disclosed.

**Primary CTA**

> WHY THIS MATTERS

**Secondary CTA**

> VIEW THE EVIDENCE

**Evidence badge pattern**

> [CONFIDENCE] · [SOURCE TYPE]

Examples:

- HIGH CONFIDENCE · PRIMARY SOURCE
- REPORTED · ORIGINAL DOCUMENT NOT LINKED
- ESTIMATED · DERIVATION SHOWN

## “Why this matters” drawer

Use exactly these headings:

1. WHAT HAPPENED
2. WHY IT MATTERS
3. WHAT CHANGED
4. WHAT HAPPENS NEXT
5. WHAT COULD BLOCK IT
6. VIEW THE EVIDENCE

Aim for one or two sentences per section. The drawer is a summary of existing records, never a new factual source.

## Investor-watch panel

**Title**

> WHAT INVESTORS ARE WATCHING

Prioritise up to three real items:

1. next dated delivery or guided window
2. customer acceptance or billing evidence
3. remaining contracted capacity
4. financing or long-lead dependency
5. next earnings/event catalyst

State should be one of:

- On track
- Unresolved
- Pending evidence
- Date unknown

## Newcomer explainer

**Eyebrow**

> 60-SECOND EXPLAINER

**Title**

> HOW AN AI DATA CENTRE MAKES MONEY

Suggested sequence:

1. Electricity reaches the site.
2. Cooling and power systems support the computing equipment.
3. Servers run customer workloads.
4. The customer tests and accepts the contracted deployment.
5. Revenue begins when the contract's billing conditions are met and disclosed.

Final caveat:

> Acceptance and billing are different stages. T2C does not assume one proves the other.
